# Question sources

The bank contains 100 questions: Korean history (28), science (24), geography (24), and arts and culture (24). Each has a complete English translation, including choices, explanation, and hint. Question IDs and answer positions are shared between languages.

All 100 answers were reviewed against institutional publications on November 18, 2026. Both language files carry matching `sources` and `verifiedAt` fields. References support the answer and its scope; the files do not reproduce the publishers' question wording. Where a website blocks direct access, available publisher search text or a publisher-hosted PDF was used. URLs can change after this review.

IDs 1–52 were retained from the earlier project and audited. No original ID, choice order, or correct-answer index changed. The review clarified the seven-continent model, Goryeo's main capital, the 676 Battle of Gibeolpo, equatorial planetary diameter, absolute zero, and the simplified equation for oxygenic photosynthesis. The Nile question now asks about its Mediterranean outlet instead of relying on a disputed length comparison. Vatican City's unnecessary area figure was removed.
