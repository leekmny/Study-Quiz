# Design notes

The app keeps short practice sessions, immediate explanations, and a quiet layout at its center. All interface text and questions are available in Korean and English.

Ideas reviewed:

- [Anki](https://github.com/ankitects/anki) and its [study manual](https://docs.ankiweb.net/manual/studying): revisit missed material and keep practice manageable. This app uses a simple latest-answer review list, not Anki's scheduling algorithm.
- [quizzz](https://github.com/SamVia/quizzz): a separate practice path for incorrect answers.
- [Quizsense](https://github.com/SQLicious/Quizsense-ai): a date-seeded daily challenge and subject-level progress.

These informed feature choices. No code, question bank, branding, or visual assets were copied from those projects.
